Research
I am a particle physicist, working on the phenomenology and the measurement of a double parton scattering in hadron-hadron collisions. I am currently a member of the ATLAS Collaboration at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) in CERN near Geneva. The LHC machine accelerates and collides mostly protons, which compose of smaller components called partons. I am especially interested in the events where two or more pairs of partons interact and produce high transverse momentum particles.
